POLICY ENDORSEMENT: WAIVER OF SPECIFIC DISEASE AND PED WAITING PERIODS
Endorsement Reference: END-2024-WAV-001
UIN: SYNTH-END-WAV-001/V1/2024
Attached to and forming part of Policy Series: HealthGuard Corporate & Retail Endorsed Plans

SECTION 1 — ENDORSEMENT CLAUSE AND REVERSAL OF BASE EXCLUSIONS
1.1 Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in Section 3 (Waiting Periods) of the
Base Policy Wording or Benefit Schedules:
The 24-month Specific Disease Waiting Period (applicable to Cataract H26, Hernia K40-K46,
Cholelithiasis/Gallstones K80, Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy N40, and Knee Arthroplasty M17)
is hereby EXPRESSLY WAIVED, DELETED, AND REVERSED in its entirety from policy inception.

1.2 Waiver of Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:
The standard 48-month Pre-Existing Disease (PED) waiting period is hereby waived for all declared
covered conditions under this special corporate endorsement.

1.3 Conflict of Terms & Priority of Construction:
In the event of any conflict, discrepancy, or inconsistency between the terms of this Endorsement
and the Base Policy Wording or general benefit tables, the provisions of this Endorsement shall
strictly supersede, govern, and reverse the base policy exclusions.
